## Required external verdict

The reviewer must mark each provisional claim as **DISTINCT**, **PARTIALLY ANTICIPATED**, **KNOWN**, or **UNRESOLVED**, with a primary citation and a one-paragraph comparison. A single relevant paper that directly anticipates the strict family, shell certificate, or stopping theorem must be reported even if it weakens the manuscript.
