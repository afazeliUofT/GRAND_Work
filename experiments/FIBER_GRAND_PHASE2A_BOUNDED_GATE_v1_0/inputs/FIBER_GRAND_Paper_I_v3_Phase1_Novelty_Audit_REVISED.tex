\section{Frozen purpose}
\label{sec:purpose}

\subsection{Single research question}

Paper~I will answer one narrow question:

\begin{quote}
\emph{Can an exact, code-agnostic GRAND-style decoder perform maximum-likelihood decoding of arbitrary short binary block codes on a channel with exactly one deletion and independent substitutions by aggregating all deletion alignments supporting each candidate and stopping with a rigorous unseen-candidate certificate?}
\end{quote}

The paper must establish both correctness and computational relevance. Exact ML agreement alone is insufficient because exhaustive codeword-wise ML is already exact. Computational speed alone is insufficient unless every reported exact decision is accompanied by a valid mathematical stopping argument.

\subsection{Frozen one-sentence contribution}
\label{sec:claim}

\begin{freezebox}[Provisional conference claim]
On a binary channel with exactly one deletion and independent substitutions, individual-history first-hit guessing can strictly disagree with aggregate maximum likelihood; FIBER-GRAND instead performs duplicate-aware inverse-alignment search, exactly scores discovered codewords, and terminates only when an unseen-candidate upper bound certifies the global ML decision.
\end{freezebox}

The final abstract may add a computational-advantage statement only after the quantitative gate in \cref{sec:gates} has passed. Until then, words such as ``efficient,'' ``substantially faster,'' or ``low-complexity'' are hypotheses rather than results.

\section{Frozen channel, code, and decision model}

\subsection{Channel law}
\label{sec:channel}

The transmitted word is
\[
X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\in\{0,1\}^n.
\]
Independent substitutions act as
\[
\widetilde X_i=X_i\oplus N_i,
\qquad
N_i\stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p_s),
\qquad 0\le p_s<\tfrac12.
\]
Exactly one position
\[
J\in\{1,\ldots,n\},
\qquad
\Prb[J=j]=q_j,
\qquad
q_j\ge0,
\qquad
\sum_{j=1}^n q_j=1,
\]
is deleted, giving
\[
Y=\Del_J(\widetilde X)\in\{0,1\}^{n-1}.
\]
The decoder knows $n$, $p_s$, and $\{q_j\}_{j=1}^n$. Uniform deletion, $q_j=1/n$, is the primary theorem and experiment setting. A known nonuniform deletion law may appear only as a compact robustness check and must not expand the main story.

The equivalent interpretation ``delete first and substitute only surviving coordinates'' is permitted because the substitution on the deleted coordinate marginalizes to one.

\subsection{Code model}

The code is an arbitrary, unmodified binary block code
\[
\C\subseteq\{0,1\}^n
\]
with a uniform prior over codewords. Correctness uses only the interface
\[
\texttt{IsCodeword}(x)\in\{\texttt{true},\texttt{false}\}.
\]
Parity checks, syndromes, prefix tests, or other code-specific structures may be used only as optional implementation accelerators. No theorem of exactness may depend on redesigning the code for deletion correction.

The scalar decoder output is the lexicographically smallest member of the global ML tie set. Certification tests must additionally retain the complete ML tie set.

\subsection{Exact decision objective}

For a candidate $x$ and observation $y$, the transformation fiber is the collection of deletion/substitution histories that map $x$ to $y$. Its aggregate likelihood is
\[
\Lambda_y(x)=W(y\mid x).
\]
The exact decision is
\[
\widehat c_{\mathrm{ML}}(y)
\in
\argmax_{c\in\C}\Lambda_y(c).
\]
Paper~I concerns exact hard-decision ML only. Approximate decoding, abandonment, erasures, lists, and soft outputs are outside the conference scope.

\section{Frozen mathematical core}

\subsection{Exact one-deletion likelihood}

For $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ and $y=(y_1,\ldots,y_{n-1})$, define the mismatch count for deletion alignment $j$ by
\begin{equation}
 d_j(x,y)=
 \sum_{i=1}^{j-1}\ind\{x_i\ne y_i\}
 +\sum_{i=j+1}^{n}\ind\{x_i\ne y_{i-1}\}.
 \label{eq:dj}
\end{equation}
The aggregate likelihood is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\Lambda_y(x)=
\sum_{j=1}^{n}q_j
p_s^{d_j(x,y)}(1-p_s)^{n-1-d_j(x,y)}.}
\label{eq:likelihood}
\end{equation}
At $p_s=0$, the Bernoulli term is interpreted by its ordinary indicator/limit convention.

All alignment mismatch counts must be computed in $O(n)$ time using
\begin{align}
 d_1(x,y)
 &=\sum_{i=2}^{n}\ind\{x_i\ne y_{i-1}\},\\
 d_{j+1}(x,y)
 &=d_j(x,y)
 -\ind\{x_{j+1}\ne y_j\}
 +\ind\{x_j\ne y_j\}.
 \label{eq:recurrence}
\end{align}
Consequently, exact scoring of one codeword costs $O(n)$ time and $O(1)$ auxiliary memory beyond the stored words.

\subsection{Required strict failure result}

A tie-dependent example is not sufficient as the headline result. The conference paper must use a strict best-path/aggregate-ML reversal.

\begin{proposition}[Strict best-path/aggregate-ML reversal]
\label{prop:strict}
Assume uniform deletion, $q_j=1/n$, and $0<p_s<1/2$. For $n\ge4$, define
\[
y=0^{n-3}10,
\qquad
x_A=0^{n-3}101,
\qquad
x_B=0^n,
\qquad
\C=\{x_A,x_B\}.
\]
The most probable individual compatible history strictly favors $x_A$:
\[
M_y(x_A)=\frac1n(1-p_s)^{n-1}
>
\frac1n p_s(1-p_s)^{n-2}=M_y(x_B).
\]
However, aggregate ML strictly favors $x_B$ whenever
\begin{equation}
np_s>1+2p_s^2.
\label{eq:reversal-condition}
\end{equation}
Thus, for every fixed $p_s>0$, a sufficiently large finite blocklength produces a strict disagreement between best-path first-hit ordering and aggregate ML ordering.
\end{proposition}

The exact aggregate expressions are
\begin{align}
\Lambda_y(x_B)
&=p_s(1-p_s)^{n-2},\\
\Lambda_y(x_A)
&=\frac1n\Bigl[
(1-p_s)^{n-1}
+p_s(1-p_s)^{n-2}
+p_s^2(1-p_s)^{n-3}
+(n-3)p_s^3(1-p_s)^{n-4}
\Bigr].
\end{align}
Their strict ordering reduces to \cref{eq:reversal-condition}.

\begin{proof}
For $x_B=0^n$, every deletion alignment leaves exactly one mismatch with $y$, hence $d_j(x_B,y)=1$ for all $j$ and the displayed expression for $\Lambda_y(x_B)$ follows. For $x_A$, the mismatch counts are $0,1,2$, and $3$ with multiplicities $1,1,1$, and $n-3$, respectively. This gives the displayed expression for $\Lambda_y(x_A)$. The best individual history for $x_A$ has zero surviving substitutions, whereas every compatible history for $x_B$ has one, so $M_y(x_A)>M_y(x_B)$ for $0<p_s<1/2$. Direct factorization gives
\[
 \frac{n\bigl(\Lambda_y(x_B)-\Lambda_y(x_A)\bigr)}{(1-p_s)^{n-4}}
 =(1-2p_s)\bigl(np_s-1-2p_s^2\bigr).
\]
The first factor is positive on the assumed range; therefore aggregate ML strictly favors $x_B$ exactly when \cref{eq:reversal-condition} holds. For every fixed $p_s>0$, an integer $n>(1+2p_s^2)/p_s$ exists. The restriction $n\ge4$ avoids a vacuous negative exponent in the displayed aggregate formula and covers every blocklength at which the reversal condition can hold under $p_s<1/2$.
\end{proof}

The algebra above establishes mathematical correctness of the family. Phase~1 only supports the narrower novelty statement that this explicit coding formulation and threshold were not located in the targeted search; the general best-path/best-string phenomenon is prior art.

\subsection{Boundary lemma}
\label{sec:boundary}

For a fixed observation $y$, any individual-history enumerator induces an order on distinct candidates according to the first supporting history emitted. First-hit decoding is ML for every codebook if and only if that induced order is nonincreasing in $\Lambda_y(x)$, with a declared tie convention. This is retained as a short boundary lemma, not as the sole foundational theorem.

\subsection{Unseen-candidate certificate}

For deletion position $j$, substitution pattern $e\in\{0,1\}^{n-1}$, and deleted input bit $b\in\{0,1\}$, define
\[
x=\Ins_j(y\oplus e,b)
\]
with component weight
\[
\omega_j(e)=q_jp_s^{w_H(e)}(1-p_s)^{n-1-w_H(e)}.
\]
Each alignment stream emits $(e,b)$ in nonincreasing $\omega_j(e)$. Let $\eta_j(t)$ be the largest not-yet-emitted component weight in stream $j$ after search step $t$, or zero if the stream is exhausted.

\begin{theorem}[Independent-stream exact certificate]
\label{thm:certificate}
Every completely undiscovered candidate satisfies
\[
\Lambda_y(x)\le U_t^{\mathrm{new}}:=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\eta_j(t).
\]
Let $\D_t$ be the set of distinct candidates already generated. Assume every discovered codeword is exactly scored and that at least one codeword has been discovered, $\C\cap\D_t\ne\varnothing$. Define
\[
s_t^\star=\max_{c\in\C\cap\D_t}\Lambda_y(c)
\]
and
\[
\mathcal T_t^\star=
\{c\in\C\cap\D_t:\Lambda_y(c)=s_t^\star\}.
\]
If
\[
s_t^\star>\sum_{j=1}^{n}\eta_j(t),
\]
then $\mathcal T_t^\star$ is the complete global ML tie set. If all streams are exhausted, the same conclusion holds without the strict inequality.
\end{theorem}

\begin{riskbox}[Novelty discipline]
The independent-stream threshold in \cref{thm:certificate} is a transparent correctness baseline. Sorted-list thresholding and generic residual-frontier ideas have substantial prior art. Paper~I must not claim that the bare sum $\sum_j\eta_j$ is independently a new generic search principle. The coding contribution must rest on the strict synchronization-channel failure result, the inverse-alignment construction, exact code-modular ML certification, and preferably a channel-specific frontier refinement or nontrivial complexity theorem.
\end{riskbox}

\subsection{Required channel-specific strengthening}
\label{sec:strengthen}

For an ISIT-strength submission, Paper~I should contain at least one result beyond the independent-stream certificate. The preferred target is an alignment-consistent upper bound that uses
\[
 d_{j+1}(x,y)=d_j(x,y)
 -\ind\{x_{j+1}\ne y_j\}
 +\ind\{x_j\ne y_j\}
\]
to rule out mutually incompatible combinations of per-stream frontier maxima.

The accepted strengthening may be one of:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item a polynomial-time alignment-consistency bound that is provably no looser than $\sum_j\eta_j$ and is strictly tighter for a characterized family;
  \item an optimality result showing that the independent-stream certificate is tight under a precisely stated oracle/model restriction;
  \item a nontrivial finite-blocklength complexity theorem for the exact one-deletion search.
\end{enumerate}

If none is obtained, the work may still be considered for ITW only if novelty survives Phase~1 and the exact computational advantage is unusually strong.

\section{Frozen decoder architecture}

\subsection{Reference algorithm}
\label{sec:refalg}

The conference reference decoder is the discover--membership-test--exact-score variant:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Initialize one probability-ordered inverse-alignment stream for each deletion position $j$.
  \item Repeatedly emit the globally largest next component $(j,e,b)$ and form $x=\Ins_j(y\oplus e,b)$.
  \item Canonicalize $x$ and suppress duplicate candidate processing.
  \item On first discovery, call \texttt{IsCodeword}$(x)$.
  \item Only when $x$ is a codeword, compute the complete aggregate score \cref{eq:likelihood} using \cref{eq:recurrence}; then update the incumbent and discovered tie set.
  \item Stop only when the incumbent score is strictly larger than $\sum_j\eta_j$, or when every stream is exhausted.
\end{enumerate}

Testing membership before exact likelihood evaluation is frozen because noncodewords cannot compete in the ML maximization. This preserves exactness while avoiding unnecessary $O(n)$ scores. The implementation must still count every distinct candidate and every membership call so that the computational comparison remains transparent.

\subsection{Frozen workload definitions}

Every run must record
\begin{align*}
Q_{\mathrm{hist}}&=\text{number of emitted history components},\\
Q_{\mathrm{disc}}&=\text{number of distinct candidate words generated},\\
Q_{\mathrm{code}}&=\text{number of code-membership calls},\\
Q_{\mathrm{score}}&=\text{number of exact aggregate codeword scores},\\
D_{\mathrm{dup}}&=1-Q_{\mathrm{disc}}/Q_{\mathrm{hist}},\\
M_{\mathrm{peak}}&=\text{peak frontier, cache, and score memory},\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{stop}}&=s_t^\star-\sum_j\eta_j\quad\text{at certification}.
\end{align*}
Wall-clock time must include stream generation, hashing, membership, scoring, and certification. It must not report only the likelihood kernel.

\section{Frozen conference evidence package}

\subsection{Exactness certification}

Before performance claims, the following binary tests must pass:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item verify \cref{eq:likelihood,eq:recurrence} against an independently written exact rational-arithmetic oracle;
  \item enumerate all codebooks for $n\le4$ and all observations $y\in\{0,1\}^{n-1}$;
  \item enumerate all two-codeword codebooks for $n\le8$ and all observations;
  \item test representative linear and nonlinear codes through $n\le16$ over all observations where feasible and exhaustive random channel realizations otherwise;
  \item verify zero ML disagreements, zero incorrect tie sets, and zero false stopping certificates;
  \item explicitly include long runs, alternating words, edge deletions, repeated symbols, and score ties.
\end{enumerate}

Exact arithmetic is the release oracle. Floating-point or log-domain implementations must be compared against it, and near-zero stopping margins must trigger higher precision rather than an uncertified decision.

\section{Submission gates and decision rules}
\label{sec:gates}

\begin{gatebox}[Gate P0 --- scope freeze: PASS upon issuance of this document]
The paper studies only the declared one-deletion+BSC model, exact hard-decision ML, membership-only code modularity, the strict failure theorem, the exact inverse-alignment decoder, certification, and the frozen evidence package. Deferred subjects remain excluded.
\end{gatebox}

\begin{gatebox}[Gate P1 --- novelty]
The exact strict-reversal family appears unanticipated in the targeted literature, but the general path-versus-string phenomenon, aggregate alignment likelihood, threshold stopping, membership-oracle modularity, and expensive-predicate filtering are credited as prior art. Gate~P1 is only a \emph{provisional continuation gate}; conference-level novelty is not passed unless Gate~P5 supplies a synchronization-specific theorem or bound beyond those generic ingredients.
\end{gatebox}

\begin{gatebox}[Gate P2 --- mathematics]
The strict-reversal theorem, likelihood identity, recurrence, and stopping certificate pass independent manual and symbolic proof review under explicit assumptions and tie rules.
\end{gatebox}

\begin{gatebox}[Gate P3 --- exact software]
All frozen complete-world and adversarial tests produce zero ML disagreements, zero tie-set errors, and zero false certificates. This gate is binary.
\end{gatebox}

\begin{gatebox}[Gate P4 --- computational viability]
For at least three unmodified code families and a contiguous nondegenerate operating region, the decoder achieves both:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item at least $100\times$ reduction in median exact codeword-likelihood evaluations relative to exhaustive ML; and
  \item at least $10\times$ reduction at the 99.9th percentile,
\end{enumerate}
with a material end-to-end wall-clock gain after membership, hashing, and frontier overhead are included. These are research gates, not presently claimed results.
\end{gatebox}

\begin{gatebox}[Gate P5 --- conference-strength specialization]
At least one channel-specific strengthened bound, nontrivial complexity theorem, or comparably strong structural result survives novelty review and measurably improves the scientific story beyond a direct application of generic threshold aggregation.
\end{gatebox}

\begin{stopbox}[Decision rule]
\textbf{ISIT GO:} P1--P5 all pass.\par
\textbf{ITW GO:} P1--P4 pass, P5 is partial, and the strict theorem plus empirical gains remain compelling.\par
\textbf{NARROW:} correctness passes but gains occur only in a clearly identified high-rate/low-$p_s$ regime; submit only with that restricted claim.\par
\textbf{STOP or fundamentally reframe:} novelty is absorbed by prior art, the certificate is near-exhaustive across useful regimes, tail complexity is uncontrolled, or exact end-to-end speedup is absent.
\end{stopbox}

\end{itemize}

These exclusions are not abandoned research topics. They are deferred to later papers so that the first conference submission has one falsifiable mathematical and algorithmic story.

\subsection{Decisive prior-art findings}

\begin{stopbox}[Finding 1 --- the independent frontier is not the contribution]
For each deletion position $j$, the map $(e,b)\mapsto\Ins_j(y\oplus e,b)$ is a bijection, so every candidate has exactly one local component in every alignment list. Its aggregate likelihood is the sum of those local grades. Consequently, $\sum_j\eta_j$ is the standard monotone sorted-list frontier bound of threshold aggregation \cite{fagin2003ta}. The manuscript must credit this explicitly and avoid claims of a new generic certificate.
\end{stopbox}

\begin{stopbox}[Finding 2 --- the membership filter is also not independently new]
A code-membership call is an expensive Boolean predicate applied to ranked candidate objects. Prior top-$k$ work explicitly studies expensive predicates that require per-object probes and derives probe-minimizing algorithms \cite{chang2002mpro}. GRAND itself establishes membership-only code modularity for additive channels \cite{duffy2019grand}. The scientific opportunity is therefore not ``membership-first search,'' but whether synchronization geometry supports a materially tighter bound or a coding-specific oracle theorem.
\end{stopbox}

\begin{stopbox}[Finding 3 --- the v2 package verdict was too strong]
The absence of a single paper containing the exact same assembly is not enough to establish novelty. N7 is removed from the pass logic. As of this audit, N1 is the only apparently distinct completed result, and it is too narrow by itself to guarantee an ISIT-strength contribution.
\end{stopbox}

\subsection{Alignment-consistent Phase-2 theorem target}
\label{sec:ac-target}

The audit produced a concrete route beyond generic threshold aggregation. It is included to make the continuation decision falsifiable; it is \emph{not} counted as an established novel contribution in Phase~1.

Let $m=n-1$ and define
\[
 g_j(d)=q_jp_s^d(1-p_s)^{m-d}.
\]
For each nonexhausted stream $j$, let $r_j$ be the mismatch shell of its current frontier, so $\eta_j=g_j(r_j)$. Set $r_j=n$ for an exhausted stream. Because $p_s<1/2$, $g_j(d)$ is nonincreasing in $d$ for every $q_j>0$.

Every completely undiscovered candidate must satisfy $d_j(x,y)\ge r_j$ for all $j$. Define the shell-relaxed feasible set and its exact channel-specific bound by
\begin{align}
 \mathcal U_{\mathrm{sh}}(y,\mathbf r)
 &=\{x\in\{0,1\}^n:d_j(x,y)\ge r_j,\;j=1,\ldots,n\},\\
 U_{\mathrm{AC}}(y,\mathbf r)
 &=\max_{x\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm{sh}}(y,\mathbf r)}
   \sum_{j=1}^n g_j\bigl(d_j(x,y)\bigr),
\end{align}
with the maximum of the empty set defined as zero.

\begin{proposition}[Alignment-consistent shell bound]
\label{prop:ac}
Every completely undiscovered candidate has likelihood at most $U_{\mathrm{AC}}(y,\mathbf r)$, and
\[
 U_{\mathrm{AC}}(y,\mathbf r)
 \le \sum_{j=1}^n g_j(r_j)
 =\sum_{j=1}^n\eta_j.
\]
Moreover, $U_{\mathrm{AC}}$ can be evaluated exactly in $O(n^4)$ time and $O(n^3)$ rolling memory by dynamic programming.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch sufficient for the Phase-1 handoff]
If a candidate had $d_j<r_j$ in any stream, its component in that stream would already have been emitted, so it would be discovered. Monotonicity of $g_j$ then gives the independent-bound inequality. For exact evaluation, guess $a=d_1$ and scan $x_1,\ldots,x_n$. At stage $j$, a state $(a,b,d,h)$ stores $b=x_j$, $d=d_j$, and
\[
 h=\sum_{i=2}^{j}\ind\{x_i\ne y_{i-1}\},
\]
with value equal to the largest accumulated score $\sum_{k=1}^{j}g_k(d_k)$ among prefixes reaching that state. Initialize $(a,b,a,0)$ for $b\in\{0,1\}$ whenever $a\ge r_1$. Choosing $c=x_{j+1}$ gives
\begin{align*}
 h'&=h+\ind\{c\ne y_j\},\\
 d'&=d-\ind\{c\ne y_j\}+\ind\{b\ne y_j\},
\end{align*}
retain the transition only when $d'\ge r_{j+1}$, and add $g_{j+1}(d')$. At $j=n$, accept only states with $h=a$, which enforces the guessed value of $d_1$. The three integer state coordinates have $O(n)$ possibilities, the bit coordinate has two, and there are $n$ stages and two transitions per state, yielding the stated bounds.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Strict improvement on a characterized family]
\label{prop:ac-strict}
Assume uniform deletion, $y=0^{n-1}$, and that every stream has completed mismatch shell zero but no component from shell one has been emitted, so $r_j=1$ for all $j$. Then
\begin{align*}
 U_{\mathrm{AC}}
 &=\frac{2}{n}p_s(1-p_s)^{n-2}
   +\frac{n-2}{n}p_s^2(1-p_s)^{n-3},\\
 U_{\mathrm{ind}}
 &=p_s(1-p_s)^{n-2},\\
 U_{\mathrm{ind}}-U_{\mathrm{AC}}
 &=\frac{n-2}{n}p_s(1-p_s)^{n-3}(1-2p_s)>0.
\end{align*}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For $y=0^{n-1}$, $d_j(x,y)=w_H(x)-x_j$. The constraint $d_j\ge1$ for every $j$ is equivalent to $w_H(x)\ge2$. For weight $w$, the aggregate score is
\[
 \frac1n\bigl[w\,g(w-1)+(n-w)g(w)\bigr],
 \qquad g(d)=p_s^d(1-p_s)^{n-1-d}.
\]
Writing $\rho=p_s/(1-p_s)<1$ shows this expression decreases strictly for $w\ge2$, so the maximum occurs at $w=2$. Substitution gives the displayed formula and strict gap.
\end{proof}

\begin{riskbox}[Interpretation of the new bound]
\Cref{prop:ac,prop:ac-strict} establish a mathematically valid synchronization-specific refinement. They do not yet establish conference novelty or useful latency: the $O(n^4)$ implementation may cost more than the search it tightens. Phase~2A must independently reprove the DP, exhaustively certify it, seek a cheaper incremental realization, and measure net certified work.
\end{riskbox}

\subsection{Exact Phase-2A work package and kill gate}
\label{sec:phase2a}

Phase~2A is the next and only authorized step:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Produce a proof-only source containing \cref{prop:strict,thm:certificate,prop:ac,prop:ac-strict}, with every assumption and tie convention explicit.
  \item Implement two independent exact versions of $U_{\mathrm{AC}}$: the dynamic program above and exhaustive enumeration for small $n$. Require zero discrepancies for every $y$, broad threshold vectors $\mathbf r$, and $n\le10$ where feasible.
  \item Integrate both $U_{\mathrm{ind}}$ and $U_{\mathrm{AC}}$ into one exact decoder, preserving identical stream order, membership oracle, and tie handling.
  \item Run only a small pilot at $n\in\{16,24,32\}$, two rates, two code families, and $p_s\in\{0.01,0.05\}$. Record total wall time, $Q_{\mathrm{hist}}$, $Q_{\mathrm{code}}$, certification depth, and bound-evaluation overhead.
  \item Refresh the literature search specifically for constrained rank aggregation, correlated sorted lists, deletion-alignment bounds, and exact one-deletion ML search.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{stopbox}[Phase-2A exit rule]
\textbf{CONTINUE toward an ISIT/ITW paper} only if all proofs and exhaustive tests pass and the alignment-consistent mechanism gives a clear net benefit: either at least a $2\times$ median and $3\times$ 99th-percentile reduction in membership/search work versus the independent bound without erasing the gain in wall time, or a stronger theorem that establishes nontrivial optimality/complexity independent of those empirical thresholds.

\textbf{NARROW to an ITW-style mathematical note} if the bound is structurally new and provably strict but computationally expensive, provided the strict theorem and a meaningful complexity characterization remain.

\textbf{STOP the present Paper-I algorithmic route} if the DP is anticipated by prior art, cannot be implemented without dominant overhead, or produces negligible certification improvement across the pilot. Do not compensate by adding more channel models or more simulations.
\end{stopbox}
